A SMALL SPACE ADVENTURE

Lost far from home.

You are Nix, an alien pilot from the Veyra Galaxy. A damaged jump drive has stranded you in the Milky Way. Your map core says the only stable route home is to travel inward through this solar system, collect warp cells, and activate an ancient jump gate.

Move with WASD or arrow keys. Hold Space to boost. Reach the glowing gate to finish a level. Planet sizes use one shared scale; distances are compressed for gameplay.
